## Project Overview
The objective was to identify where profitable sales growth could be improved across:
- Sales trends
- Products & categories
- Regions
- Sales representatives
- Order value
- Discounting

## Preparation
**Before analysis,** several data-quality **issues were identified** in the raw dataset, including inconsistent text, excess whitespace, irregular values, inconsistent date formats and missing numeric values.

The data was prepared by:
- Standardising text, spacing and capitalisation
- Correcting date formats
- Converting fields to appropriate numeric data types
- Handling missing and incorrect values
- Enriching transactional data using lookup tables

## Key Findings

### Sales Trend
Sales fell by approximately **79% between June–September 2025**, compared with a **44% increase** during the same period in 2024.

### Product Performance
**Laptops generated approximately $176.1K**, making them the highest-revenue product.

### Sales Representative Performance
The **top three sales representatives accounted for approximately 54% of total sales**, with the highest-performing representative also recording a substantially higher AOV.

### Order Composition
**Low-value orders represented approximately 69.8% of total orders**, highlighting an opportunity to investigate order-value growth.

---
## Recommendations

1. **Investigate the 2025 sales decline**  
   Review the changes between **June and September 2025** to identify the causes of the decline.

2. **Benchmark high-performing sales practices**  
   Analyse the top-performing representative's approach and use relevant practices to inform targeted training.

3. **Protect laptop availability**  
   Monitor laptop inventory and increase supply when demand indicates potential stock-out risk.
